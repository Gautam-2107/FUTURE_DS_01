# Task 1 – Business Performance Analytics Dashboard using Power BI

## Objective
To analyze retail sales data and identify:
- Revenue trends over time
- Top-selling products
- High-value categories and regions
- Overall business performance using KPIs

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop

## Dataset
- Superstore Sales Dataset (CSV)

## Steps Performed
1. Imported the sales dataset into Power BI  
2. Cleaned and prepared the data (data types, dates, errors)  
3. Created calculated measures for Sales, Profit, Orders, and Profit Margin  
4. Built KPI cards to highlight key business metrics  
5. Designed visualizations for:
   - Sales trends over time
   - Category and segment performance
   - Regional sales using map visualization
   - Top-selling products
6. Designed a clean, professional dashboard layout for business users  

## Key Insights
- Sales showed a strong upward trend after 2015  
- Technology category contributed the highest revenue and profit  
- Consumer segment generated the largest share of sales  
- Certain regions consistently outperformed others in sales volume  
- Top-selling products accounted for a significant portion of total revenue  

## Dashboard Overview
The dashboard presents a complete view of business performance, helping stakeholders make data-driven decisions using clear and interactive visuals.
